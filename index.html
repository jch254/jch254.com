Who am I?

Kia ora, I'm Jordan. I'm a New Zealand software engineer focused on building scalable web platforms, cloud infrastructure, and developer tooling.

Over the past 15+ years I've worked across fintech, hospitality, legal tech, and startups. I've built APIs, distributed systems, deployment pipelines, and internal tooling. My background spans companies like Xero, me&u, and LawVu.

Lately I've been focused on AI-assisted product development, reusable architecture patterns, and shipping smaller systems quickly without sacrificing long-term maintainability. A lot of my recent work sits at the intersection of infrastructure, automation, and product engineering.

I like systems that are minimal, explicit, tenant-aware, and operationally predictable.

Outside of client and product work I maintain a handful of open-source projects and experiments. One of those is Reference Architecture, a production-ready scaffolding platform for bootstrapping modern applications and AI-assisted workflows. I also build smaller product experiments, including Lush Aural Treats, an AI-assisted music discovery platform.

After years in Melbourne and Austin I returned home to Te Waipounamu, Aotearoa in 2024 and got back on the tools.

What do I do?

I design and build systems that support real products in production.

That includes backend architecture, cloud infrastructure, automation, multi-tenant applications, developer tooling, and AI-assisted workflows. I enjoy working close to the infrastructure layer, but I also care deeply about product shape, usability, and developer experience.

A lot of my recent work has focused on reusable foundations for fast-moving teams and modern product development. The goal is to reduce friction, increase reliability, and make systems easier to evolve over time.

I'm drawn to systems that stay understandable as they scale. Clear operational boundaries. Explicit infrastructure. Predictable deployment models. Simple runtime behaviour over unnecessary complexity.
